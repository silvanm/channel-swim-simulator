Against the record book

Durations of 3,074 ratified E→F solos (CSA/CS&PF database) · dashed line = median 13h 21m · record 6h 45m · only 31.8% of real solos land on Cap Gris-Nez itself
Simplified M2 tidal-stream model of the Dover Strait (peak ≈1.9 kn mid-strait at springs, race off Cap Gris-Nez, net NE residual). Optimiser solves a Zermelo-style navigation problem by coordinate descent over start time and leg headings. Calendar mode takes HW Dover and the tidal range from an M2+S2+N2 harmonic model fitted to published Dover predictions (~15 min); clock times are Dover local (GMT/BST). Water temperature from NASA JPL MUR SST v4.1 monthly composites. Educational toy — not for navigation. · Benchmarks: English Channel Swim Database (solos, public edition).
